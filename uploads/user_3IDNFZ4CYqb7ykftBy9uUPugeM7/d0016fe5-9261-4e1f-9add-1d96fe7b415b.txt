Once upon a time, Lina found an old key under a tree in her village. The key opened a small wooden box hidden behind her grandfather’s bookshelf. Inside, she discovered a handwritten letter:

“Dear Lina, courage is not the absence of fear. It is choosing to move forward even when you are afraid.”

Lina kept the letter in her pocket. From that day, whenever she faced a difficult decision, she remembered her grandfather’s words and took one small step forward.
